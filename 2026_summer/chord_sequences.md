# Nest 2026 Chord Sequences

Source sheets: imported images in `2026_summer/`.

Format:

- Each `| ... |` cell is one bar.
- A single chord token means that chord lasts for the whole bar.
- A bracketed token, for example `[Am C]`, means multiple chords inside that same bar, in order.
- Source staff lines and labelled parts are kept on separate lines where useful.
- `?` marks an uncertain reading from a cropped or handwritten scan.
